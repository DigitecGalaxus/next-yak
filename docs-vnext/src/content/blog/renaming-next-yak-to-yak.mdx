## What this post will cover

- Why the name changed, and why now.
- What `next-yak` grew into: a build-time CSS-in-JS library for React, Solid and Qwik, not

- The new package names: `@yak/react`, `@yak/solid` and `@yak/qwik`.
- What you have to change in your project, and what keeps working untouched.
- How long the old package keeps receiving releases.
