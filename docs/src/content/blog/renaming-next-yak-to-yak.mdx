## What this post will cover

- Why the name changed, and why now. The name `next-yak` says "Next.js and React only". Both
  parts are no longer true.
- yak runs on every major bundler, not only Next.js. See
  [One compiler, every bundler](/blog/one-compiler-every-bundler).
- yak runs on React, Solid and Qwik. See [yak for Solid](/blog/yak-solid) and
  [yak for Qwik](/blog/yak-qwik).
- yak is fast. See
  [Your styled components disappear at build time](/blog/build-time-folding).
- The new package names: `@yak/react`, `@yak/solid` and `@yak/qwik`.
- What you have to change in your project, and what keeps working untouched.
- How long `next-yak` keeps receiving releases.
- The new site: new documentation, a playground for all three frameworks, and this blog.

## Migrating today

The rename guide shows the changes from `next-yak` to `@yak/react`. The
[migration guide](/docs/migration-from-styled-components) covers the move from
styled-components.
